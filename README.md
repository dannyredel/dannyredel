### About Me:

- 👋 Hi, I'm Daniel! I'm an applied economist in Berlin working on **causal inference, experimentation and measurement**.
- 💼 I work as an Analyst in Competition Economics at [Compass Lexecon](https://www.compasslexecon.com/), on cartel damages, merger control and other competition cases using large panel datasets.
- 🎓 I hold an MSc in Econometrics from Tilburg University and an MSc in Applied Economics from the Pontifical Catholic University of Chile (PUC-Chile), both with Distinction.
- 🔭 Outside of work I build open-source tools and applied projects in Python, mostly around one question: *how do you know the effect you estimated is real?*
- ✍️ I write about causal inference and econometrics on [my blog](https://dannyredel.github.io/myposts.html).
- 📫 How to reach me: dannyredel@gmail.com [![Linkedin Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/-dannyredel-blue?style=flat&logo=Linkedin&logoColor=white)](https://www.linkedin.com/in/daniel-redel-14b052b6/)

---

### 🧭 What I work on:

- **Causal inference & econometrics:** difference-in-differences (staggered adoption, event studies, HonestDiD sensitivity), synthetic control, IV, double/debiased ML, causal forests
- **Experimentation:** A/B test design, power analysis, CUPED variance reduction, interference and switchback designs, Bayesian A/B testing
- **Pricing & demand:** discrete choice (MNL, mixed logit, PyBLP), conjoint analysis, willingness-to-pay, pass-through

---

### 🚀 Selected projects:

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [**didkit**](https://github.com/dannyredel/didkit) | Python package for difference-in-differences: event-study plots with nested and simultaneous confidence bands, plus Rambachan–Roth sensitivity analysis for parallel trends. Brings R-style `ggfixest` / HonestDiD tooling to Python. |
| [**RentSignal**](https://rentsignal.de) | Rent intelligence for the German market: XGBoost quantile prediction with conformal intervals, SHAP explanations, satellite-derived location features, and propensity-score-matched estimates of renovation effects. |

### ✍️ Writing & research:

- [*Ex-Post Assessment of the Copec-CGL Retail Gasoline Merger*](https://centrocompetencia.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/COPEC-CGL-Merger-An-Ex-Post-Empirical-Assessment-of-the-FNEs-Decision.pdf) (CentroCompetencia, 2024): DiD with spatial econometrics to measure post-merger price effects.
- [*Impact of Transit-Oriented Development on Dutch Commercial Real Estate Prices*](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1eexmSSJQyfKPfWoaVycenRmXK9wTZ7Jw/view?usp=sharing) (MSc thesis, CPB / Tilburg, Distinction): staggered DiD (Sun-Abraham), nonparametric treatment-effect estimation.
- [Recent blog posts](https://dannyredel.github.io/myposts.html): interpreting event studies across modern DiD methods · DiD vs. synthetic control vs. synthetic DiD · sensitivity analysis for parallel trends · causal forests and heterogeneous treatment effects
